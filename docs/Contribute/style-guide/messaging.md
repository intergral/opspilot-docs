# Describing OpsPilot

This page is the reference for how we describe OpsPilot in the docs - the positioning, the category, and the language rules. Apply it whenever you write about the product.

!!! note "The key shift"
    System health remains the outcome. **Continuous operational understanding** is what sets OpsPilot apart.

---

## Core messaging

| Element | Wording |
|---|---|
| **Slogan** | From telemetry to continuous operational understanding. |
| **Core promise** | Understand what matters. Act with confidence. Verify the outcome. |
| **Main insight** | Your team should not have to spend its day interpreting telemetry just to understand what matters. |
| **Positioning line** | OpsPilot is an AI observability platform that continuously interprets telemetry, connects evidence, and shows teams what needs attention, why it matters, and what to do next. |
| **Category** | AI observability platform with continuous AI-assisted investigation built in. |
| **The shift we sell** | From telemetry, dashboards, and alerts to continuous operational understanding. |
| **Outcome** | Less manual investigation. Healthier systems. |
| **Customer benefit** | Spend less time investigating monitoring data and more time resolving problems. |

---

## The four questions OpsPilot answers

Frame features around the question they help answer:

1. **What needs attention?**
2. **Why does it matter?**
3. **What should we do next?**
4. **Is the situation improving?**

---

## Brand architecture

- **OpsPilot** is the platform and the primary brand.
- **Coworker** is the AI capability built into OpsPilot that enables continuous investigation and rechecking.

Always build recognition for **OpsPilot**. Coworker is a capability of the platform, not a separate product, so lead with OpsPilot and describe Coworker as part of it.

---

## Language rules

| Rule | Guidance |
|---|---|
| **Retired** | "Observability that keeps your systems healthy" as the lead line - "system health" is too generic to own. |
| **Retired** | "From dashboards to answers" - use the telemetry-to-understanding shift instead. |
| **Retired** | "Faster and easier than ever before" - too generic. |
| **Changed** | "What is the right fix?" becomes **"What should we do next?"** - this avoids overclaiming. |
| **De-emphasised** | "AI SRE" - crowded category language that can overstate Coworker. |
| **Retired** | "Assistant" - always use **"Coworker"**. |
