# Budget & cost

The **Budget & cost** tab is a single **Coworker spend** card that controls how much Coworker can spend each month and what happens as it approaches the limit. It covers tasks, webhooks, heartbeat, and chat; catalog maintenance is budgeted separately.

![!Screenshot](../../../../../Coworker/budget-cost.png)

| Control | Description |
|---|---|
| **Your plan allowance** | Your organisation's total monthly AI Token allowance (read-only) |
| **On-demand usage** | **On / Off**, off by default. **Off** makes your plan allowance a hard ceiling - Coworker pauses new work until the period renews, and nothing bills as on-demand. **On** lets Coworker keep working past the plan allowance, up to your monthly spend cap |
| **Monthly spend cap** | The most Coworker may spend each month, in tokens. The panel shows the approximate number of investigations that buys per month, alongside your recent monthly average for comparison. Pick a preset (**50%**, **80%**, or **Plan**) or drag the slider; set it to **Plan** and Coworker stops exactly at your plan allowance |
| **Warning threshold** | Sends a notification when spend reaches this percentage of the cap. Pick a preset (**5%** - **100%**) or drag the slider |

!!! warning "On-demand usage can exceed your allowance"
    With **On-demand usage** on, Coworker can spend past your plan allowance, up to your **Monthly spend cap**. Set the cap to bound the extra cost.

Click **Save** to apply your changes. See [AI Tokens](../usage.md#ai-token-allowance) for full usage details and optimisation suggestions.
